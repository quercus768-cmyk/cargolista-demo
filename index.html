THE PLATFORM

HOW IT WORKS

Cargolista is a B2B platform that provides an environment for shippers, consignees, freight
forwarders and logistics companies to find partners, publish transportation requests and
communicate directly.

01
01

PUBLISH & EXPLORE

Looking for a forwarder? Publish the request through your dashboard or our Support Desk.

Looking for orders? Browse available RFQs.

02
02

CONNECT & DISCUSS

Communicate directly on the platform in Request Chats.

Use the Live Desk for general questions, clarifications and establishing connections.

03
03

PROVIDE OFFERS

Send and receive offers by email or directly in your dashboard for easier submission, viewing, sorting and evaluation.

REGISTRATION, ACCESS TO RFQS (POSTING AND ANSWERING) AND DIRECT COMMUNICATION ARE FREE FOR ALL USERS.
